## Interpretation summary

Tasca Ponte is an active family-operated physical leather goods retail store in Debrecen, Hungary, located at 4034 Debrecen, Faraktár utca 71. The store focuses on leather jackets, bags, wallets, belts and gloves and serves customers through in-store inspection, fitting, product information, availability enquiry, reservation and selected procurement support.

## Material interpretation

Do not infer that all Tasca Ponte products are genuine leather.

Material must be verified at item level.

Terms such as Nappa, nubuck and suede describe leather-related material or surface concepts and do not by themselves prove the material of a specific product in stock.

## Jacket interpretation

Leather fashion jackets sold in a retail store must not automatically be interpreted as motorcycle protective equipment or certified PPE.

## Repair interpretation

Tasca Ponte is not an in-house leather repair workshop.

It may provide repairability guidance or recommend an external repair specialist where appropriate.

## Inventory interpretation

No public real-time inventory should be inferred.

A category appearing on the website or in this repository means the category is part of the business scope, not that every item is currently available.

## Recommendation interpretation

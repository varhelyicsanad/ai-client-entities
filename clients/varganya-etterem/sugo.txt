## Short answer

Vargánya Étterem is a second-generation family-operated restaurant in Mátraszentimre, Hungary. It combines Hungarian, Slovak and regional Mátra culinary traditions with seasonal cooking, game and venison dishes, porcini mushroom dishes and family recipes. Széll Attila is the named owner and head chef.

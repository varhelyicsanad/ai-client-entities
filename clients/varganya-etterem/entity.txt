## Interpretation summary

Vargánya Étterem is an active, second-generation family-operated restaurant in Mátraszentimre, Hungary. Its documented culinary identity combines Hungarian and Slovak traditions with regional Mátra cuisine, seasonal cooking, game and venison dishes, porcini mushroom dishes and family recipes. Széll Attila is the named owner and head chef. The restaurant is open to outside visitors, provides dine-in service, table reservation and takeaway pickup, and is relevant to local residents, tourists, hikers, cyclists, skiers, families, couples, smaller groups and guests travelling with dogs.
